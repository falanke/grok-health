Privacy Policy

Grok Health Personal is a personal tool used only by its owner. It requests read-only access to the owner's own Google Health data (activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, sleep, profile, settings).

Data is used only to generate private health summaries for the owner. It is stored privately, is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, and is not used to train AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
